# Changelog

Notable changes to Best Gear Setup. Versions match `version=` in `runelite-plugin.properties`, which the
Plugin Hub displays.

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

First Plugin Hub submission.

- Best melee, ranged and magic setup for any monster or boss, searched from the side panel or by
  right-clicking an NPC, in *Owned items only*, *Owned + budget* or *Best in slot* mode.
- Owned items from your bank, inventory and equipment, remembered per account, with manual ownership,
  slot locks and exclusions.
- DPS calculator following OSRS Wiki formulas, including encounter rules, raid scaling, boss phases,
  special attacks, AoE, damage over time and whole-fight HP effects. See [COMBAT_AUDIT.md](COMBAT_AUDIT.md).
- Mandatory slayer protection (facemasks, earmuffs, nose pegs, mirror shields, Karuulm boots and others)
  and dragonfire protection are enforced as gear constraints.
- Bundled OSRS Wiki snapshots (retrieved 1 October 2026 UTC); the plugin makes no network requests.
