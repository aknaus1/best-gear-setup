## Message 1 — Getting started

# Best Gear Setup • Quick start
Find gear for your next monster or boss without leaving RuneLite.

**First-time setup**
1. Open RuneLite's **Plugin Hub**, search **Best Gear Setup**, and install it.
2. Log in and **open your bank once** so the plugin can see what you own.
3. Open the **Best Gear Setup** sidebar panel. You can also right-click an NPC and choose **Best setup**.
4. Search for your target and select the correct monster version or phase.
5. Choose a search mode, check your fight settings, and click **Find best setup**.

**Which mode should I use?**
• **Owned items only** — build around gear you own.
• **Inventory + equipped only** — work with what you are carrying and wearing.
• **Owned + budget** — use owned gear and consider purchases within your budget (for example, `50m`).
• **Best in slot** — explore the strongest options without an ownership or budget restriction.

Start with **Owned items only** for a practical setup, then try **Owned + budget** when planning upgrades.

## Message 2 — Using your results

# Get useful recommendations
**Check the assumptions first**
• **Fight**: review HP, Slayer task, raid party size, Wilderness settings, and pre-fight defence reductions where relevant.
• **Settings**: review attack styles, equipment options, spellbooks, boosts, search options, and highlights.
• **Gear**: review your locked items, excluded items, and extra items marked as owned.

**Make the setup fit your account**
Right-click an item in a result to **lock it**, **exclude it**, or **mark it as owned**. You can also manage these rules from **Gear**. Re-run the search after changing your choices.

**Read the whole result**
Compare the suggested gear, DPS, accuracy, max hit, estimated time to kill, and the cost of items you would need to buy. Check alternatives when you prefer another item. The bank gear layout and highlights help you find the setup's gear, runes, and potions.

**Use estimates as a guide**
Results describe expected damage under the selected assumptions. They do not simulate a complete fight: movement, switch timing, overkill, and line of sight are not modelled. A small DPS advantage may be less useful to you than a setup you can use comfortably.

## Message 3 — FAQs and support

# FAQs
**Why is gear I own missing?**
Open your bank on the account/profile you are using, then search again. Check the mode: **Inventory + equipped only** limits the search to carried and worn gear. Review exclusions and other equipment settings, and use **Gear** to mark extra items as owned when needed.

**Why is it suggesting something I do not own?**
Check your mode. **Owned + budget** can suggest purchases; **Best in slot** is not limited to owned items. Review any items you manually marked as owned.

**Why does the result look different from another calculator or guide?**
Match the monster version/phase, stats, prayers, boosts, spellbook, Slayer task, raid scaling, special attacks, and defence reductions. Different assumptions can change the recommendation.

**Does it automatically equip gear or fight for me?**
Use the recommendations and bank highlights to choose and withdraw your setup; you remain in control of your gear and combat.

**Is my bank uploaded?**
The search runs locally with bundled data. The plugin documentation states that it makes no network requests; prices come from RuneLite.
